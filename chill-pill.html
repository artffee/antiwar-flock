Read the card, then start the timer.

An audible laugh scores 0. Smiling is allowed. Each friend gets one silly question.

COMPOSURE: QUESTIONABLE / VICTORY: YOURS

THE ONLY RULE: KEEP IT TOGETHER

  1. Draw + read. Get into character before the timer starts.
  2. Commit to the bit. Perform for the full 30 seconds while your friends ask silly questions.
  3. Score + pass. No laugh: 1 point. Laugh or pass: 0. Pass the phone and the pill clockwise.
How do we win?

First to 5 points triggers the final round. Finish that round so everyone gets equal turns. Highest score wins. Tied leaders each take an extra turn; repeat until one leads.

What if we run out of cards?

The 36 challenges reshuffle after you play them all. You can pass any card for 0 points. Keep questions verbal and about the fictional scene.
