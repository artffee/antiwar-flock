Your privacy.
Plain language.

This notice describes the current A305X website and its optional features. Updated September 30, 2026.

Website visitsOptional submissionsSharing & servicesBrowser storageYour choices

Website visits.

A305X uses Vercel to host the website. When your browser requests a page or file, hosting infrastructure processes technical information such as the IP address, requested URL, and browser information to deliver and protect the service.

Some pages load fonts or game images from third-party websites. Those providers receive the technical information needed to serve the requested files. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Information you choose to submit.

You can browse the collection without creating an account. Optional features may process information you provide:

  • Email sign-ups: if you use a sign-up feature, your email address and sign-up time are stored.
  • Shareable Flock messages: the recipient name, message, chosen character, creation time, and a share identifier are stored so the message can be opened from its link.
  • AI character chat: if used, recent messages are sent to Google’s Gemini service to generate a reply. Do not enter sensitive information.

The website’s submission endpoints use hashed IP identifiers and request timestamps for abuse prevention.

Shareable content & service providers.

Anyone with the link to a Flock message can read it. A link is not an authenticated private mailbox. Share only information you are comfortable making available to the people who receive or forward that link.

Hosting and optional submitted records use Vercel services. AI replies, when available, use Google’s Gemini service. If a product purchase is enabled, payment details are entered on Stripe’s hosted checkout; A305X does not provide a card-entry form on this site.

Browser storage.

Games can save progress and settings in your browser’s local storage. This lets you return to a game on the same browser. Clearing website data in your browser removes those locally saved records.

The current site does not include an advertising tracker or analytics script. This notice will be updated if the website adds services that change how information is used.

Your choices.

Optional features are optional: you can browse without signing up, chatting, or sharing a message. Avoid including private contact details in shareable content.

Technical logs and submitted records are retained by the services used to operate the website; there is no published fixed deletion schedule. Customer-support and privacy-request contact details are available on the support page as they are established.

For a request about information you submitted, contact . Include enough context to identify the record, but do not send payment credentials.
