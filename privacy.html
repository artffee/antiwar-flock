Your privacy.
Plain language.

This notice describes the current A305X website and its optional features. Updated October 29, 2026.

Website visitsOptional submissionsSharing & servicesBrowser storageYour choices

Website visits.

A305X uses Vercel to host the website. When your browser requests a page or file, hosting infrastructure processes technical information such as the IP address, requested URL, and browser information to deliver and protect the service.

Some pages load fonts or game images from third-party websites. Those providers receive the technical information needed to serve the requested files. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Information you choose to submit.

You can browse the collection without creating an account. Optional features may process information you provide:

  • Email sign-ups: we privately store your email address, signup time, original and current consent wording/version/date, requested product interests, confirmed email preferences, and subscription/verification status. When email delivery is enabled, a signup request can trigger an address-confirmation email. You must open its link, choose your topics and confirm before receiving marketing. Old or unconfirmed signups are not automatically added to campaigns. Email links use signed tokens; confirmation links expire after 24 hours. We store confirmation-token hashes and limited delivery/abuse records, including provider message IDs, attempt times and delivery or failure status. We do not enable email open/click tracking as part of this system. You can unsubscribe on the signup page or use the opt-out in an email without an account. We retain minimal unsubscribe and suppression records to prevent future messages; repeated signup does not clear a block.
  • Shareable Flock messages: the recipient name, message, chosen character, creation time, and a share identifier are stored so the message can be opened from its link.
  • AI character chat: if used, recent messages are sent to Google’s Gemini service to generate a reply. Do not enter sensitive information.

The website’s submission endpoints use hashed IP identifiers and request timestamps for abuse prevention.

Shareable content & service providers.

Anyone with the link to a Flock message can read it. A link is not an authenticated private mailbox. Share only information you are comfortable making available to the people who receive or forward that link.

Hosting and optional submitted records use Vercel services. When email sending is configured, Resend processes recipients, email content and delivery events to send confirmation messages, record delivery outcomes, and manage bounces, complaints and provider suppression. Marketing delivery remains disabled in this release. A configured email provider does not by itself mean an address is subscribed or a message was received. AI replies, when available, use Google’s Gemini service. If a product purchase is enabled, payment details are entered on Stripe’s hosted checkout; A305X does not provide a card-entry form on this site.

Browser storage.

Games can save progress and settings in your browser’s local storage. This lets you return to a game on the same browser. Clearing website data in your browser removes those locally saved records.

The site has Google Analytics 4 and Vercel Web Analytics integrations prepared, but both are currently disabled. No Google Analytics script is loaded and no analytics data is sent in this state. If Google Analytics is activated, it will be optional: you can allow or decline it and change your choice using Analytics settings. The prepared integration honors Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control, measures only allowlisted public page views, strips query strings and fragments, omits referrers, and excludes shareable messages, form contents, and email addresses. Analytics cookies will be used only after permission; the browser will also remember your choice. The site does not use advertising trackers. Product interests you explicitly select are stored with your email-list preferences, separately from visitor analytics. This notice will be updated when analytics collection is enabled.

Your choices.

Optional features are optional: you can browse without signing up, chatting, or sharing a message. Avoid including private contact details in shareable content.

We keep active subscription and consent records to manage the updates you request. We retain existing subscription records and hashed unsubscribe/suppression identifiers to prevent accidental re-enrollment; unsubscribe does not automatically delete personal fields. Delivery and security records support troubleshooting and abuse prevention; there is no automatic deletion schedule enabled. You may request access, correction or removal of personal fields through the contact below. Minimal suppression identifiers may be retained to honor your opt-out. A message already dispatched before an unsubscribe may still arrive. For privacy requests, contact orionmind@gmail.com or visit our support page.

For a request about information you submitted, contact . Include enough context to identify the record, but do not send payment credentials.
