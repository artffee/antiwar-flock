A305X is an independent art and merchandise brand created by Ivaylo Peytchev. Born in Miami, with an anti-war spirit and a very loud flamingo.

Our storyWhat we makeOur anti-war message

Art with a point of view.

A305X brings original flamingo artwork into clothing, printed objects, notebooks, and playful ideas. The character is curious, rebellious, and unapologetically human.

Our work mixes Miami color, surreal humor, and a simple belief: creativity and kindness deserve more space than war and division.

From artwork to everyday objects.

The current collection includes the Wear the Message Tee, the Chill Pill art-object concept, and the Department of Surrender notebook concept. Each product page distinguishes artwork and mockups from available products.

Physical products are still in development. Orders and preorders are not open. Our online games and the Chill Pill print-and-play are available to explore now.

Check the store for current availability, product details, and prices in USD.
