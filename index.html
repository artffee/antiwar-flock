Manifesto for the beautifully unmanageable

The system has enough beige.

A305X is not here to fight machines. It is here to interrupt automatic living. We believe peace can be loud, humor can be armor, and a ridiculous bird can tell the truth better than another serious man in a serious suit.

RULE 01

Question the default.

If everybody is marching in one direction, check who printed the map.

RULE 02

Keep your humor.

The moment power becomes ridiculous, it loses half its costume.

RULE 03

Make peace visible.

Wear what you believe. Leave a bright signal in a gray room.
