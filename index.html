The Manifesto
“We cannot stop every war. But we can refuse to profit from silence.”

A305X started as three friends drawing angry birds on the back of a merch order form. It became a flock. Every drop is small, numbered, and gone fast — because the point was never to build a fashion label. It was to move money to people who need it, loud enough that you can't scroll past it.

How It Works

BUY. WEAR.
FUND.

01

You buy the drop

Each release is capped and numbered. When it's gone, it's gone — no restocks, no filler sizes added later.

02

You wear the refusal

The flock isn't subtle. It's a piece of streetwear built to start the conversation you were going to have anyway.

03

Profit funds relief

100% of profit is wired directly to vetted humanitarian partners providing medical aid and shelter in conflict zones.
